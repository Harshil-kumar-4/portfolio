Cost Optimization → FinOps Agent Architecture

  • Manually audited live compute infrastructure and executed a targeted rightsizing initiative that reclaimed $13,400+ in annual cloud spend (~40% reduction on audited resources), building hands-on judgment for safe, telemetry-informed rightsizing.
